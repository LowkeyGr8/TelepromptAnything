Questions people ask first

Does it really work offline?

Yes. The tool is one file. After you download it, it opens and runs without a connection. The fonts and the Word reader are bundled in the file you receive.

Which devices can I use?

Anything with a modern browser: Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone or iPad. On a phone, save the file to your device and open it from there.

Is there a subscription or a renewal?

No. You pay once. Updates are free.

Do you see my scripts?

No. The file reads your script on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. The demo above lets you run 3 takes with your own script. Because this is a digital download, all sales are final once you have paid.

Can I use it with a physical teleprompter rig?

Yes. Turn on mirror mode and the text flips for a beam splitter glass.
